# mygames

Simple browser games, hosted on GitHub Pages.

Play: https://ubuildmore.github.io/mygames/

- **Pong**: you vs. the computer. W/S or arrow keys (or drag on touch screens), Space to pause, first to 7 wins.
- **Gravity Hop**: take off, fly and land a spaceship against gravity across 5 missions with rising gravity, wind and rock formations. ←/→ or A/D rotate, ↑/W thrust (on-screen buttons on touch screens). Land slow, upright and with both feet on the green pad.
- **Orbit Runner**: pilot a rocket around a tiny planet with real orbital gravity across 5 levels of asteroid belts and a moon. Collect every crystal, then land gently on the green pad (it also refuels you). ←/→ or A/D rotate, ↑/W thrust (on-screen buttons on touch screens). The dotted line predicts your path.
